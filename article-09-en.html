Truck and Heavy Equipment Filters: What Makes Them Different?

Mada Filters & Automotive Oils — Syria

Trucks and heavy equipment operate under radically different conditions than passenger cars — heavier loads, longer run times, and a harsher work environment. This directly shapes the filter specifications required.

Size and filtration capacity

Truck engines are larger and consume more oil and air, requiring filters and strainers with higher filtration capacity and pressure tolerance.

A harsher work environment

  • Greater dust exposure, especially on construction or agricultural sites.
  • Longer continuous operating hours.
  • Higher mechanical load stressing the lubrication system.
  • Shorter inspection intervals even with quality filters.

Mada's advice

We stock REXA and MANLEOU filters and strainers for Volvo and Scania trucks and heavy equipment, and our team can help fleet owners identify the correct part.
