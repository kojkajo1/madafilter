Summer Car Care in Syria: Filters and Oils to Check

Mada Filters & Automotive Oils — Syria

Summer heat puts extra strain on your engine and quickly exposes any neglected maintenance.

Engine oil first

High heat accelerates oil breakdown and reduces viscosity. Check the level and color, and don't delay a scheduled change past summer.

Air filter and cabin filter

Syrian summers usually mean more dust. A clean air filter keeps combustion efficient, and a clean cabin filter keeps cooling effective.

Quick checklist

  • Engine oil and oil strainer.
  • Air filter and cabin filter.
  • Fuel filter if you notice heat-related stumbling.
  • Coolant level and belts.
