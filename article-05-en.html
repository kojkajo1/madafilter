Cabin Air Filters: Why They Affect Your Health and AC Performance

Mada Filters & Automotive Oils — Syria

The cabin air filter is one of the most overlooked maintenance items, despite its direct role in the air quality you and your passengers breathe every day.

What it does

It filters dust and pollutants from air entering the cabin. A clogged filter forces the AC system to work harder for the same cooling result.

Signs it needs replacing

  • Noticeably weaker airflow from the vents.
  • An unpleasant smell when the AC runs.
  • Visible dust buildup on the dashboard.
  • A slight rise in fuel use from AC compressor strain.

Mada's advice

Seasonal dust across Syria fills cabin filters faster than average — we recommend checking every 10,000–15,000 km.
