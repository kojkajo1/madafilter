What Is an Oil Cartridge and How Is It Different From a Regular Oil Filter?

Mada Filters & Automotive Oils — Syria

The term oil cartridge confuses a lot of car owners, especially alongside terms like insert-type oil cartridge. This article breaks down the difference simply.

Cartridge vs. full spin-on filter

A traditional spin-on filter is a complete unit including the metal housing, filter media, and valves, fully replaced each time. A cartridge is just the internal filtering element (media + rubber seals) — the outer housing stays fixed on the car and never changes.

Why do some cars use a cartridge instead of a full filter?

  • Less metal waste — no housing to discard at every change.
  • Engine design — some manufacturers (Hyundai, Kia) use this system as standard.
  • Relatively lower cost for the cartridge itself compared to an equivalent-quality full filter.
