German vs. Korean Engine Oil: Which Suits Syria's Climate?

Mada Filters & Automotive Oils — Syria

Choosing the right engine oil directly affects engine life, especially in Syria's climate, which combines high summer heat with clear winter cold in certain regions.

German Mitanol oils

German oils are known for strict manufacturing standards and formulas that hold their viscosity under high heat. Mitanol, a global brand we represent as official agents, is engineered for consistent protection even in extreme heat.

Korean X-Oil

X-Oil from Korea is known for modern formulas that balance performance and price, well suited to newer engines requiring precise viscosity grades.

How to choose

  • Driving type — daily city driving vs. long distances and heavy load.
  • Engine age and condition.
  • Budget vs. change interval.
  • The car manufacturer's recommendation.

Mada's advice

As official agents for both brands, our team can help you determine the right oil for your specific vehicle and usage.
