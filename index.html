About Mada

Built on Supply Chains, Not Shortcuts

Mada Filters was founded in Idlib, Syria, by a team with deep roots in the auto parts and supply chain trade. We saw a clear gap in the market: a need for filtration products that combine consistent quality with reliable availability — so we built a company around exactly that.

From day one, we've focused on long-term relationships with clients and suppliers, and on a distribution network that gets products where they're needed, fast. Today, Mada continues to expand its product range and distribution footprint.

To become the leading filtration solutions company in Syria, and build a trusted brand that extends across Middle East markets through quality, innovation, and long-term partnerships.
