Filter & Strainer Guide for the Most Common Hyundai and Kia Models in Syria

Mada Filters & Automotive Oils — Syria

Hyundai and Kia are among the most common vehicle brands in the Syrian market, for both personal and commercial use. This guide summarizes the key filtration parts we stock for each popular model.

Hyundai Santa Fe

For the Santa Fe we stock a double-valve oil strainer, a 2010 Santa Fe cartridge, and a 2005 Santa Fe air filter — near-complete coverage depending on model year.

Hyundai Porter

For the Porter we stock a 10mm ceramic diesel strainer and a Porter air filter, well suited to this model's heavy commercial use.

Hyundai Veracruz

We stock a Veracruz oil cartridge and a Veracruz air filter, matching original engine specifications.

Kia Rio

We stock a Kia Rio cartridge, along with air filters for other Kia engines such as the Kia 4000 series.
